About Knight's Garage

Knight's Garage is an independent sim-racing performance project focused on track-specific setup development, competitive testing, driver coaching, and custom iRacing liveries.

Setups are developed through repeated testing and telemetry analysis, with the goal of finding a balance between outright lap time, consistency, tire behavior, and drivability.

The goal is simple: build it, test it, measure it, and make it faster.
