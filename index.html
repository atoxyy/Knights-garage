About Knight's Garage

Built around performance.

I'm Ryan Knight, a mechanical engineering graduate with a background in vehicle development, motorsport, and hands-on engineering.

Knight's Garage started as an independent project focused on applying the same engineering mindset used in vehicle development to sim racing.

Setups are developed through repeated testing, telemetry analysis, vehicle behavior evaluation, and track-specific optimization.

The goal is simple: understand the car, find the performance, and build something that works.
