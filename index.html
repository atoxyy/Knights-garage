      About

      I'm Ryan Knight, a mechanical engineering graduate with a background in CAD/CAE design and race engineering — including work as a race engineer in endurance racing in the WRL. Knight's Garage started because I kept building sim setups for myself that were faster than anything I could find online, and it's grown into a place to show real engineering work alongside it: 3D-printed sim racing hardware and CAD/CAE consulting.

      Everything here is built the same way I'd approach a real car: test it, measure it, adjust it, and only hand it off once it's actually right. The lap time videos aren't marketing — they're the same proof I'd want to see before trusting a setup myself.
